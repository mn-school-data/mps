# About this site

This site sets out, as clearly and accurately as we can, the data behind Minneapolis Public Schools and the public schools around it: where the money comes from and goes, how enrollment is changing, the buildings, test results and college paths, and the other schools many Minneapolis families choose. The goal is a shared set of facts that parents, staff, board members, taxpayers and reporters can all look at, so that conversations about what comes next can start from the same numbers.

## How it is put together

- **Public sources only.** Figures come from the district's audited financial reports, budget books and board materials, Minnesota Department of Education data, state licensing and property records, federal filings and press coverage. Each page lists its sources, and the [sources page](sources.html) collects them.
- **Our estimates are labeled.** Where a number is our own calculation or grouping, the text says so and explains how it was made.
- **Open questions are listed.** When the public record does not answer a question, the page says what data would.
- **The data is downloadable.** Every table behind the charts is listed in the [Data](about.html#data) section below as CSV.

## Corrections

Spot a factual error, an out-of-date figure or a better source? Please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/mn-school-data/mps/issues) with the page, the claim and a link to the source. Corrections are welcome, and fixes appear in the next update of the site.
