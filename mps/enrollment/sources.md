# Sources: MPS enrollment vs Minneapolis population

## MPS enrollment
- **ADM (average daily membership), FY2015-FY2024**: MPS audited Annual Comprehensive Financial Report FY2024, MD&A Table A-4 "Student Enrollment (Average Daily Membership)". Includes Pre-K and kindergarten ("Reg K, Pre-K & KH"). FY2024 = 2023-24 school year.
  https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1743703187/mplsk12mnus/r5aztdqr2tnfysimaa9l/FinancialStatements2024.pdf
- **October 1 K-12 headcount**:
  - 2023-24 28,086 (official), 2024-25 28,912 (official), 2025-26 28,827 (preliminary): MPS budget site, https://budget.mpschools.org/budget/enrollment_summary
  - 2019-20 33,593 and 2022-23 28,473 (MDE K-12): https://www.mplsschoolsvoices.news/posts/minneapolis-public-schools-enrollment-declines-in-line-with-spring-budget-forecast
  - 2021-22 29,580 and "down about 7.6%" from 2020-21: Star Tribune, Nov 25 2021, https://www.startribune.com/minneapolis-st-paul-schools-enrollment-consolidation-harding-gun/600120766 . 2020-21 value in the CSV is derived from that percentage (Center for School Change reports 31,841 for 2020-21; the count basis is not stated in that source, so it is not used here).
- Federal NCES count for 2024-25 is 29,928 (includes Pre-K), https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/districtsearch/district_detail.asp?ID2=2721240

## Minneapolis population
- Census Bureau Population Estimates Program, July 1 estimates, as tabulated at https://www.everycityintheusa.com/cities/minneapolis-mn/ : 2015-2019 from Vintage 2019, 2020-2025 from Vintage 2025. V2025 2025 = 430,324 confirmed on Census QuickFacts. V2019 2019 = 429,606 matches the MPS ACFR. The two vintages are not strictly comparable (2020 census recalibration).
- MN State Demographic Center's own estimate is higher (2024: 435,233); Met Council estimates show growth since 2020 where Census shows a dip.

## School-age children (point data only; no clean annual series retrieved)
- ACS 2020-2024 5-year: 57,413 residents aged 5-17 (13.4%), 23,030 under 5.
- ACS 2019-2023 5-year: 81,570 under 18.
- MPS district analysis: ~12,000 fewer children living in Minneapolis in 2023 than 2019 (mplsschoolsvoices.news).
- MPR News (Mar 2024): >55,000 children under 9 in 2019, <43,000 in 2022.
- Share of Minneapolis-resident public-school students attending MPS (MDE, via mplsschoolsvoices): 63% in 2019-20, 58% in 2022-23; charters ~26% -> ~28%; open enrollment out ~12% -> 14%.
